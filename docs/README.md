## 📊 Campaign Summary

### Budget & Goals
```
Monthly Budget: 30,000₽
Daily Budget: 1,000₽

Expected Results:
├── Leads: 25-30/month (CPL ~1,000₽)
├── Students: 7-9/month (30% conversion)
├── Revenue: 140,000-180,000₽/month
└── ROAS: 4.7:1 to 6:1

LTV:CAC Ratio: 42:1 (EXCEPTIONAL)
```

### Campaign Structure
```
Ad Group 1: Exam Preparation (ОГЭ/ЕГЭ)
├── Budget: 50% (15,000₽/month)
├── Keywords: 14 high-intent exam keywords
└── Focus: Students preparing for exams

Ad Group 2: Long-Tail Keywords
├── Budget: 35% (10,500₽/month)
├── Keywords: 20 specific, low-cost keywords
└── Focus: Budget-conscious, grade-specific searches

Ad Group 3: Moscow-Specific
├── Budget: 15% (4,500₽/month)
├── Keywords: 7 geo-targeted keywords
└── Focus: Moscow & Moscow Oblast priority
```

### Why This Will Work

1. **Excellent Economics:** 42:1 LTV:CAC ratio vs 3:1 industry benchmark
2. **High-Intent Focus:** Prioritizes exam prep keywords (urgent need)
3. **Budget-Efficient:** Long-tail strategy captures low-CPC keywords (30-80₽)
4. **Geographic Focus:** Moscow priority aligns with business goals
5. **Strong Tracking:** Full conversion funnel monitoring via Yandex Metrica

## ⚠️ Common Issues & Solutions

### High spend, few leads
→ See YANDEX_DIRECT_CAMPAIGN.md "Red Flags" section
→ Add more negative keywords
→ Focus on exact match only

### Low impressions
→ Increase bids by 30-50%
→ Add broader phrase match keywords
→ Expand geographic targeting

### Good CTR, no conversions
→ Test form functionality
→ Verify tracking is working
→ Check page load speed (<3s)
